Learn hanja the playful way

Hanja2Di helps school children learn hanja and four-character idioms (사자성어) a little every day — following school worksheets and the levels of the Korean Hanja Proficiency Test.

  • Flashcards, stroke-order writing, words and quizzes
  • Levels 8급 to 1급, idioms and sayings
  • Your own sets — for example, from a school worksheet
  • Spaced repetition brings cards back just as you start to forget
  • No ads, no sign-up, no personal data collected
